## Opening

Hey, good morning/afternoon (Customer Name)?

Hey (Customer Name), this is **\_\_\_**, I'm with America's Health on a recorded line. We were just giving you a quick call today about your food and utility card that you never received. That card, it IS exclusively for members who have Medicare Parts A and B. You DO still have your Medicare Parts A and B, correct? (Let them confirm)

- Yes - (continue)
- No - ‘There must have been a mistake. Thank you for your time, have a great day.’ (Code as DQ)
- I don’t know - ‘I understand. Well, do you have the red, white and blue card by chance?’ (If yes, follow up with) ‘Okay, perfect, so then you DO have Medicare Parts A and B, correct?’ (If yes, skip to ‘Do you receive Medicaid’)

## Double-confirm the card

Okay, perfect, and JUST to double confirm, that IS the red, white and blue card, correct? (Let them confirm)

- Yes - (continue)
- I don’t have that, I have \_\_\_\_ card - ‘Okay, no problem, but you DO have your Medicare number, correct?’ (If yes, continue. If no, read the No line below.)
- No / I’m not giving my number out / I lost it - ‘Okay, you WOULD have to know your Medicare number or Social Security number to review those increases. You don't need to give it to me, but the licensed agent may need it. Do you know either of those?’ (Either way, continue)

## Medicaid

Perfect, and do you receive Medicaid along with your Medicare plan?

- Yes/No - (continue)

## State and ZIP

Perfect, thanks so much for confirming that for me. So yeah, it DOES look like you may be ENTITLED to some additional food benefits since the food prices have gone up. If that's sent, I DO still have you out in (state), with the zip code (zip code), correct?

## Work / VA insurance

Okay, LAST question here, do you have insurance through your work or the VA?

- Yes - ‘Oh, there must have been a mistake. Thank you for your time, have a great day.’ (CODE AS DQ)
- No - (continue)

## Ask for the transfer

Perfect, well that is EVERYTHING I need. So, a Medicare specialist is coming on the line now to review the additional food benefits for you. Give them JUST a few minutes to go over them with you, okay? - AFFIRM (STOP AND WAIT. The consumer MUST say yes before you click transfer)

- Yes - (Now click transfer and continue)
- No - (Do NOT click transfer. Read an EXTRA NOT INTERESTED rebuttal, then go back to the ask) ‘Give them JUST a few minutes to go over them with you, okay?’

## Cold transfer

Alright, so you'll hear a little bit of ringing, that's just me connecting them in, and once they pick up they'll ask you for your name, okay? (You already have their permission to transfer. You do NOT need to ask for another okay here or once the licensed agent connects.)
In the meantime, how's that weather out there today? (FLUFF AWAY)

# Compliance Guidelines

- Make sure you say you're on a recorded line with America's Health.
- Make sure you double confirm Medicare Parts A and B.
- Make sure you confirm ZIP code and state.
- Make sure you verify they don't have insurance through an employer or the VA (retirement/military/etc.).
- Make sure you tell them they are being transferred to a specialist.
- Wait for verbal confirmation to transfer. You MUST ask for the okay and you must wait for permission. This is the ONLY time we are asking for permission in the call!
- Stop talking once the agent connects; they will usually ask who they're speaking with. Once the lead responds, complete the transfer process. Remember, we are doing 'cold transfers' only!
- May and maybe are your best friends! Make sure you ALWAYS say may or maybe when mentioning something the lead may qualify for!
- Stick to the script. Going off script and freestyling is where compliance is broken the most. Stay scripted.
- Busy / call me back: rebut once. If they say busy again, end the call and code as Not Interested.

# Definitions (from the doc)

- **Medicare**: federal health insurance from the U.S. government. ALL people 65 and older qualify, plus people under 65 with certain disabilities. It helps cover hospital and medical care.
- **Medicaid**: a state and federally funded program run by each individual state that provides health coverage to people with limited income, with benefits varying by state.
- **Medicare Advantage plan**: a private insurance plan approved by Medicare that bundles Medicare Parts A and B, usually includes prescription drug coverage, and often offers extra benefits like dental, vision, and hearing.
- **Medicare Supplement plan**: private insurance that works with Original Medicare to help pay out-of-pocket costs like copays and deductibles.

# Operations (from the training slides — no campaign or staff names)

- **The job**: call consumers, confirm Medicare Parts A & B, double-confirm with the red, white & blue card, confirm Medicaid, verify state and ZIP, verify no insurance through work or the VA, tell them you're transferring to a licensed Medicare specialist, transfer (no introduction), stay silent, confirm both parties speak, complete the cold transfer.
- **Goal**: minimum 4 transfers per hour; top performers consistently exceed it.
- **Schedule**: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST Mon–Thu, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST Friday. Paid morning meeting 8:50 AM EST on Zoom; on the phones at 9:00. Lunch 1:00–2:00 PM EST (mandatory). Restroom breaks brief — over 5 minutes gets you logged out. POWER HOURS: no restroom breaks during the first and last hour of the day, or the hour before and after lunch.

## Dispositions

- **Answering Machine** – voicemail or ringing with no answer; someone else answers and says they're not there.
- **Dead Air** – you call their name twice at the beginning of the call but get no response.
- **Not Interested (NI)** – they acknowledge you when you greet them but hang up at any point after.
- **Hung-Up Transfer** – they hang up during or after starting the transfer process.
- **Robo** – fake automated voice, not a real person.
- **Transfer** – lead and agent connect & speak (cold transfer completed).
- **Wrong Number** – person says you have the wrong number.
- **No English** – caller cannot speak English.
- **DNC (Do Not Call)** – they tell you not to call (in any way), use profanity, or threaten you. Try to read the DNC script if possible.
  - **Management rule (2026-09-14), overrides the line above:** swearing by itself ("why the f\*\*\* are you calling me?") is an objection. Stay respectful, rebut, and keep going. It's DNC only when they tell you to stop calling / never call again / take them off the list, or threaten you ("never call me again or I'm gonna sue your ass").
- **DQ (Disqualified)** – no Medicare Parts A & B; military coverage (VA, Tricare, Tricare for Life, CHAMPVA); insurance through work/retirement; covered by a Native American tribe.
- **Conference Ended** – every transfer attempt results in the agent's line disconnecting, a busy signal, or number-not-in-service.
- **No Agent** – 5 transfer attempts, waiting at least 20 seconds on each, and still can't reach an agent.

## No agent / scheduled callbacks

1. Initial call: 5 transfer attempts, at least 20 seconds each. If you can't reach an agent, schedule a callback at least 15 minutes out.
2. First scheduled callback: full reconfirmation every callback; 5 attempts × 20 seconds. If the lead doesn't pick up, schedule the 2nd callback at least 15 minutes out (still counts as an attempt). If the lead hangs up or DNCs you, callbacks stop and you code accordingly.
3. Second scheduled callback: same rules; schedule the 3rd and final callback at least 15 minutes out.
4. Third and final callback: same rules; if still no pickup or no agent, code as No Agent.

## Call handling — zero tolerance

**Call avoidance**: staying in wrap-up/unavailable without approval; not greeting the lead within a reasonable time; hanging up when leads connect / cherry-picking; extending call time unnecessarily; staying on dead calls or answering machines too long; failing to rebuttal when the lead objects; failing to rebuttal separate objections; any action intended to delay or avoid calls.
**Improper call handling**: profanity or inappropriate language; being rude or unprofessional (volume, tone, word choice); patterns of miscoding / mishandling transfers / no agents; failing to follow compliance guidelines.

## Convoso dialer basics

- Convoso only runs in Google Chrome. Copy the Convoso link from the campaign channel, log in, allow the microphone ("Allow while visiting the site").
- Select Medicare Outbound campaign and set your status. NEVER use Not Ready.
- Statuses: Available (take calls), Break (≤5 min, not in power hours), Training (only when a manager pulls you), Personal Callback (for scheduled callbacks), Lunch (1–2 PM only), Logout (end of shift).
- Transfer: press the transfer button; the red X next to a line disconnects that line (use it to drop a failed agent attempt and press transfer again). Press "Leave Conference Call" once you've heard both parties speak, then disposition as Transfer.
- When a lead hangs up you get a pop-up — click wrap-up and disposition.
- To pause after a call, pick the status from the dropdown before dispositioning.
- Edit customer info in the Contact tab → scroll down → Apply Changes; click the Script tab to return.
- Callback disposition requires a callback time: click the blank space next to time → pick date, hour, time → set type to Personal Callback → disposition as Callback.
- Making a scheduled callback: set Personal Callback status → History tab → select the call → click the green phone icon in the primary phone field.
