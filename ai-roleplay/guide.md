
Tonality: Sounds upbeat, friendly, and confident, not robotic or bored
Pace: Speaks clearly and not too fast, especially with seniors
Strong opener: Grabs attention in the first few seconds before the caller hangs up
Filler words: Keeps "um," "uh," and "like" to a minimum
Dead air: No awkward silences, especially after an objection
Listening: Doesn't talk over the caller and lets them answer
Quick objection handling: Brushes off "not interested" or "I'm busy" smoothly and keeps the call alive
Call control: Keeps the call moving toward the transfer without getting sidetracked
Qualifying accuracy: Gets the qualifying info right before transferring
Transfer handoff: Sets up the transfer warmly so the caller stays on the line
Composure: Stays calm and polite with rude or confused callers
