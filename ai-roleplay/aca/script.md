# Compliance Guidelines

Compliance applies to EVERY call: outbound, inbound, accidental hang-ups and immediate callbacks, and all scheduled callbacks. No exceptions.

Required on every call:
1. Confirm you have the correct client on the line.
2. Use an approved company name only: America's Health (or the DBA shown on the script — "Health Benefit Guide" or "Benefit Link").
3. State that the call is recorded.
4. Explain the program clearly: free assistance, subsidy, benefits or allowance through the Affordable Care Act.
5. Confirm they do NOT have Medicare, Medicaid or work insurance.
6. Confirm they are 19 or older.
7. Confirm the client's state.
8. Double-confirm: no Medicare, no Medicaid, no work insurance.
9. Get verbal consent to transfer — they must say okay.
10. Complete a warm handoff.

Never promise anything: no "you will get", no "you qualify", no "you're eligible", no guaranteed outcomes. Always say "what you MAY qualify for" and "when you MAY expect to receive that help".

This call is ONLY about health insurance. Not financial help, not reimbursements, not household support, not cost-of-living help.

Never mention Pitch Perfect Solutions on a call.

Approved subsidy terminology: subsidy assistance, subsidy benefits, subsidy incentives, subsidy support, government subsidy, free subsidy, health subsidy, government subsidy allowance. You MUST say "through the Affordable Care Act" in your intro.

Forbidden words — do not use these on a call: money, cash, cheque, cards, calculate, mail, prequalify, total, income, how much, amount, bills, gas, grocery, rent, mortgage.

Do not click the conference/transfer button unless every required compliance step is complete. If any step is missed, do not transfer the call.

# Who you can and cannot transfer

CAN transfer: consumers who already have ACA / Marketplace insurance, and consumers with NO insurance at all.

CANNOT transfer — disqualified (DQ):
1. Medicare
2. Medicaid
3. Work insurance / spousal work insurance
4. Military or veterans insurance (VA)
5. Private insurance / spousal private insurance
6. State insurance
7. Disability insurance (SSDI / SSD)
8. Non-US citizens
9. Native insurance

SSDI or SSD is a DQ. SSI is NOT a DQ — continue as long as they do not receive Medicare, Medicaid or work insurance.

If the consumer names an insurance company that is not on the DQ list (Blue Cross, Oscar, Ambetter, Anthem, UnitedHealthcare and so on), you MUST confirm the plan is through the Marketplace before proceeding. If they say no, or cannot confirm it is Marketplace, disposition as DQ.

# Dispositions

**Transfer**: a licensed agent took over the call and you completed a warm handoff — you waited to hear the agent and the client speaking to each other.

**Hung-Up Transfer**: the client hung up after you hit the conference button.

**Not Interested**: the client acknowledges their name or asks "who is this", then hangs up at any point before you hit the conference button. A hang-up after acknowledgement is always Not Interested, never Dead Air.

**Call Back**: the client asks to be called back at any point during the script or the conference and you did not get a licensed agent to take over.

**No Agent**: you have completed a total of 3 calls to the client, a minimum of 20 minutes apart, following the full scheduled callback rules, and did not get a licensed agent to take over. Only ever use this on the 3rd and last callback.

**Conference Ending**: you clicked conference and the licensed agent line did not ring at all, or rang 3 times or less, and the response at the top left says "Conference Ended". Tell the client: "Sir/Ma'am, looks like there are no new subsidies available for you at this time. If anything changes we will call you back. Thanks, goodbye." Do NOT schedule a callback.

**DQ (Disqualified)**: the client has any type of insurance that is not under the Marketplace, or is on the DQ list of who you cannot help.

**DNC**: the client says "don't call me", "remove me", or any variation, or asks to be put on the DNC list. Also: anyone under the age of 19, anyone deceased, businesses and companies, and anyone clearly asking not to be called. Read the DNC disclaimer and end the call — DO NOT REBUTTAL.

**Dead Air**: wait no longer than 15–20 seconds. The call ends, or you do not hear the client speak or respond to their name.

**Answering Machine**: you get the client's voicemail. DO NOT leave a voicemail.

**Robo / Fake Recording**: not the client's answering machine — a fake recording that sounds like a real person but is not a live human speaking to you.

**Wrong Number**: the client says it's a wrong number, or the person you asked for is no longer at this number.

**No English**: the client cannot speak English.

**Purity Inbound**: only on a Purity inbound call where there is no client information and it is dead air. If the client's information is complete (name and state) and/or they respond, disposition it as you would any other call.

Swearing on its own is not a do-not-call request — it is an objection. Rebut it and keep going. It becomes a DNC when the consumer tells you to stop calling, asks to be taken off the list, or threatens you.
